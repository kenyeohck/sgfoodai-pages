Privacy Policy — SG Food AI

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

SG Food AI helps users track meals, calories, macronutrients, intermittent fasting windows, water intake, and weight trends. Contact us at support@komniz.com with any privacy questions.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Details: Information used to authenticate and manage your account and preferences.
  • Nutrition, Fasting & Weight Logs: Meal entries, photos submitted for nutrition estimation, fasting timers, hydration logs, and weight readings manually entered by you.
  • Subscription Status: Subscription status and purchase entitlements managed through Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide app features, estimate meal nutrition, display progress trends, manage subscriptions, and respond to support requests.

3. Data Deletion & Contact

To request account or data deletion or ask privacy questions, email support@komniz.com.
