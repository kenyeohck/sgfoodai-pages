Privacy Policy — SG Food AI

Last updated: 11 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SG Food AI (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using SG Food AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

SG Food AI collects the following information to provide our nutrition tracking services:

  • Account information — your email address and authentication credentials.
  • Health profile data — age, gender, height, weight, and activity level you provide.
  • Meal logs — photos of your meals, AI-generated nutrition estimates (calories, macronutrients), and timestamps.
  • Weight history — weight entries you log over time.

Meal photos are processed by our AI analysis provider to estimate food content and nutrition. Photos are processed in real time and are not retained by the AI provider after analysis.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the SG Food AI app:

  • Displaying your daily and historical nutrition summaries.
  • Calculating personalised calorie, macro, and hydration targets.
  • Generating charts and trends from your logged data.
  • Authenticating your account and synchronising your data across devices.

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third-Party Services

SG Food AI uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloud storage — meal photos and data are stored securely on our hosting platform.
  • AI analysis — food photos are sent to an AI model for nutrition estimation. Photos are processed in real time and not retained by the provider.
  • Authentication — we use standard authentication infrastructure to manage your login session.
  • Subscriptions — in-app purchases and subscription entitlements are processed via Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat.

Each service provider operates under its own privacy practices governing data processing.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen by selecting “Delete Account.” This permanently removes all meals, weight history, food items, and profile data. This action cannot be undone.

Upon deletion, your data is removed from our systems and cannot be recovered.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and secure authentication tokens. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

SG Food AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can update your health profile at any time within the app. To request a complete data export or deletion, contact us using the information below.

Disclaimer

SG Food AI provides AI-generated nutrition estimates for informational purposes only. These estimates are not medical advice and should not replace professional dietary guidance. Always consult a healthcare professional for personalised nutrition recommendations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy within the app. Continued use of SG Food AI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
