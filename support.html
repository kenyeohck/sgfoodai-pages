Getting Started

Set up your profile

On first launch, create your health profile — age, gender, height, weight, and activity level. This unlocks your personalised calorie, macro, and water goals.

Log a meal

Tap "Log Meal" and snap a photo of your food. Our AI detects the dishes, estimates portions, and calculates calories and macronutrients automatically.

Review & edit

Before saving, review the detected foods. You can edit any name, portion, or nutrition value if something looks off. Hit Save when you're happy.

Track your progress

The Calendar view shows a daily heatmap of your calorie intake versus your target, plus your weight trend over the month.

Delete your data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the nutrition estimates accurate?

Estimates are AI-generated and approximate, based on visual analysis of your photo and local food reference data. They are a helpful guide, not a substitute for professional dietary advice. Edit any value before saving if it looks off.

What happens to my meal photos?

Photos are sent to our AI provider for real-time analysis and are not retained by the provider after processing. Your saved meals and photos stay linked to your account until you delete them.

How is my calorie target calculated?

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation based on your age, gender, height, weight, and activity level. You can update any of these in your Profile at any time.

Can I use the app without creating a profile?

You can explore the app, but calorie targets, macro goals, and the dashboard require a profile to personalise your experience.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Your data is used only to provide the app's features to you. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How do I manage or restore my subscription?

Subscriptions billed through the App Store are managed in your Apple ID subscription settings. Tap "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen to restore an active subscription.
